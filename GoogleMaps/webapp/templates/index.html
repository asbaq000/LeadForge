Connecting your Google Sheet

  1. Create a service account at console.cloud.google.com and enable the Google Sheets API and Google Drive API.
  2. Make a JSON key for it and save the file as service_account.json in the project folder.
  3. Open your sheet, hit Share, and give the service account's email Editor access.
  4. Come back here and press Test the connection under Advanced.
